Worldpay Disputes

A 12-week MVP sprint transformed fragmented chargeback tools into a single disputes platform that helps enterprise merchants prioritize, manage, and resolve cases more efficiently.

Role

Design Director and Product Designer

Team

Researcher, 2 Designers, Account Lead

Client

Worldpay Product Team

Research Findings

Merchants were juggling multiple systems to track disputes, monitor deadlines, and gather supporting evidence. The result was lost context, missed opportunities, and limited visibility into case status.

Concept Development

A key insight helped shape the product strategy: winning more disputes isn't about working every case.

It's about quickly identifying which cases are worth pursuing and acting on them at scale. Early navigation concepts and wireframes brought the vision to life.

Design

The platform was designed around action and prioritization.

Unified case views, bulk workflows, and automation tools helped teams move faster, reduce repetitive work, and stay on top of deadlines.

We leveraged Worldpay's design system to maintain continuity across the product suite, extending it in collaboration with their UX team where needed.
